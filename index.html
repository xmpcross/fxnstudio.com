The new homepage is structured for both people and search engines: clear service pathways, internal links, crawlable headings, project proof, location relevance and direct calls to action.

FAQ

Before we build, most teams ask these.

Can FXN Studio redesign an existing website?

Yes. We can rebuild an existing site around clearer positioning, cleaner UX, improved page speed and stronger SEO foundations.

Do you work on eCommerce websites?

Yes. We design and build storefronts for better product discovery, mobile buying confidence and checkout flow.

Can SEO be included in the design process?

Yes. Search structure, headings, internal links, page speed, schema opportunities and content mapping are planned into the build.

Can the site include AI or automation?

Yes. We can connect forms, CRMs, AI agents, internal dashboards and workflow automations so the website supports operations.
